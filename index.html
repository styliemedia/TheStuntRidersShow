The show

We don't just do stunts. We perform them.

A thrilling, funny show for the whole family, with bikes, quads and a car that drives on two wheels. Everything is talked through live on the mic, so the crowd is part of the show from the first wave to the last cheer.

  • Motocross jumps

    Our super motocross riders launch off the ramp and clear a gap below, sometimes landing on a target the crowd picks out.

  • Wall of fire

    Jimbo, our head of fire, blasts through a wall of flames on a bike, and sometimes on foot when his quad won't start.

  • Two-wheel car

    James Bond-style driving: a car balanced on two side wheels whatever the weather, by the UK's top two-wheel driver on grass. One-handed.

  • Bike tricks

    Wheelies, endos and riding backwards, plus the odd quad wheelie while trying to take a selfie.

  • Jimbo's taxi

    Five to seven of the team piled onto one bike, then a quad wheelie with passengers. It doesn't always stay upright.

  • The Titanic

    The most talked-about bit of the show: Jack and Rose on top of a moving bike, arms out, while "My Heart Will Go On" plays.

Film, TV and big events

On screen and on the streets

  • London's New Year's Day Parade

    Part of the first ever stunt team to perform at the parade, on bikes, quads and in cars through central London, in front of 500,000 people.

  • Nitro Circus

    Stunts for a Nitro Circus film alongside Moto-Stunts International.

  • GoCompare

    Dan-The-Man was Phillip Schofield's stunt double, riding freestyle for a GoCompare advert.

  • Ladbible: Made It

    Filmed for a Ladbible original short, watched over 2.2 million times.

  • Music videos and albums

    Brandon doubled X Factor's Frankie Cocozza for his "Motorcycle" video, and his burnout made the Young Knives' album cover.

  • International shows

    From Bahrain International Circuit to the Muscat Festival in Oman and Rimz & Beatz in Nigeria.
