The show

We don't just do stunts. We perform them.

Bikes, quads and cars in one show, with live commentary that keeps the whole crowd shouting. You won't find another show with this much variety.

  • Motorbike jumps

    Big air on motocross bikes, right over the top of cars.

  • Fire stunts

    Riders through walls and rings of fire, the moment everyone films on their phones.

  • Two-wheel car

    A car driven balanced on two wheels around a grass arena, by the UK's best two-wheel car stunt driver on grass.

  • Quad stunts

    Wheelies, side-wheelies and handstands, often right along the barrier giving out high-fives.
